## 3. Deep Review
Four parallel read-only review lenses (`#49` Security & Governance, `#51` Architecture & Cost Control, `#50` Spec Drift, `#52` Test & Eval Quality) audited the pre-fix phase branch; fixes were implemented spec-first in `da37e4a` and independently verified by `verifier` (`#55`) and `review-writer`.

| Lens | Findings | Severity | Action (fixed / backlog / accepted) |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Security (`#49`) | `ProductOffer._validate_market_and_url` (`domain/models.py`) bypassed `matches_merchant_attribution` whenever `info.mode == "json"`, allowing unattributed/third-party Google Shopping links via `model_validate_json` without context. | High | `fixed` (`da37e4a`: JSON mode enforces Google Shopping attribution unless internal `CachedCatalogProvider` passes explicit `context={"allow_verified_google_shopping_from_cache": True}`; synced in `feature-101` `D7`/`R8`/`T3.8`). |
| Security (`#49`) | `_validate_regional_offer_url` & `_matches_noon_path` (`config/markets.py`) did not reject `.`/`..` path traversal, `%2e`/`%2f`/`%5c`/`%3b`, semicolons/backslashes, or non-locale Noon prefixes. | High | `fixed` (`da37e4a`: added `_normalize_and_validate_path_segments` and strict segment-boundary `_matches_noon_path`; synced in `feature-101` `R8`). |
| Security (`#49`) | URL validators (`config/markets.py`, `domain/models.py`, `serper_provider.py`) allowed whitespace/control chars (`\t`, `\r`, `\n`), raw `str` fallback bypassed `HttpUrl`, and `image_url` permitted embedded `user:pass@` credentials. | Medium | `fixed` (`da37e4a`: added `_validate_clean_url_input` + `has_control_or_whitespace`, removed raw `str` fallback on `ProductOffer.url`, and rejected embedded credentials in `_validate_image_url` & `_parse_https_image_url`). |
| Security (`#49`) & Architecture (`#51`) | `redact_url_password` (`config/settings.py`) left `parsed.port`/`parsed.password` unguarded, `create_cache_store` (`adapters/cache/factory.py`) called `redact_url_password` outside `try`, and query secrets (`?password=`/`?pass=`/`?token=`) and URL-encoded passwords in `extract_secret_password`/`sanitize_redis_text` (`redis_store.py`) were missed. | Medium | `fixed` (`da37e4a`: guarded all `SplitResult` reads in `try/except ValueError`, added `_mask_query_secrets`, moved `redact_url_password` inside `try` in `create_cache_store`, and added `unquote`/`quote` handling in `redis_store.py`). |
| Security (`#49`) & Architecture (`#51`) | `SerperShoppingProvider.search_offers` (`serper_provider.py`) caught only `TimeoutException`/`TransportError` (missing `HTTPError` subclasses like `DecodingError`), and `_resolve_candidate_store` called `urlsplit` outside `_parse_item`'s `try/except`. | Medium | `fixed` (`da37e4a`: added `except httpx.HTTPError` with `_redact_reason` raising `CatalogProviderError`, and wrapped `urlsplit` in `try/except ValueError` in `_resolve_candidate_store` and `_parse_https_image_url`). |
| Architecture (`#51`) | `domain/ranking.py` Stage 1 toggled each of 23 accessory patterns independently rather than by semantic concept, causing positive bilingual searches (`"Anker earbuds case"`, `"جراب أنكر"`, etc.) to reject bilingual seed offers (`"جراب ... - ... Case"`). | Medium | `fixed` (`da37e4a`: grouped patterns into 6 `_ACCESSORY_CONCEPT_GROUPS`; synced in `feature-102` `D3`/`R3`/`T1.7`). |
| Architecture (`#51`) | `InMemoryTTLCache._entries` (`adapters/cache/memory.py`) used an unbounded `dict` with zero capacity limit or `set()` sweeping, risking unbounded RAM growth under prolonged Redis fallback. | Medium | `fixed` (`da37e4a`: converted to bounded `collections.OrderedDict` with `max_entries=1024`, expired pruning, and LRU eviction; synced in `feature-103` `R2`/`T3.7`). |
| Spec Drift (`#50`) & Architecture (`#51`) | `ScoringConfig.top_k` (`ge=1`) allowed `> 3` while `RankedOffer.rank` capped at `le=3` and `ranking.py` clamped with `_MAX_RANKED_CAP=3`, causing `degraded_fewer_than_k=True` misfires. | Medium / Low | `fixed` (`da37e4a`: constrained `ScoringConfig.top_k` to `1..3` in `config/scoring.py`, removed `_MAX_RANKED_CAP` in `domain/ranking.py`, and synced `feature-101` `plan.md`/`requirements.md`/`validation.md`). |
| Test Quality (`#52`) | `test_ranking.py` and `test_phase1_e2e.py` lacked positive bilingual accessory checks (`include_accessories=False`) on bilingual titles/seed items, and E2E `include_accessories=True` test passed `"Anker earbuds case"`. | High | `fixed` (`da37e4a`: added bilingual positive accessory unit & E2E tests and tested `include_accessories=True` without `"case"` in query). |
| Test Quality (`#52`) | `"الإسكرينة ... وحماية الشاشة"` masked standalone `"حماية الشاشة"` and attached `"و"` conjunction (`"وحماية الشاشة"`), and Stage 1 unit tests omitted tashkeel/alef-maksura accessory titles. | Medium | `fixed` (`da37e4a`: added optional `و` in `domain/ranking.py` and standalone/tashkeel/alef-maksura test cases in `test_ranking.py`; synced in `feature-102` `D3`/`R3`/`T1.6`). |
